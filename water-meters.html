Key Benefits

Stop paying for water you can't see

Undetected leaks and unmetered common areas are some of the biggest hidden costs for complexes, estates, flats and shopping centres. A smart water meter from Extra Dimensions gives every stakeholder — from the body corporate to the individual tenant — a clear, real-time view of exactly what's being used.

Because every meter reports remotely over our secure IoT network, there's no need for manual readings, and abnormal usage triggers an early alert before it becomes a costly repair or an inflated bill.
