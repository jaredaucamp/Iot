Key Benefits

Know exactly where your power is going

Electricity losses and unbilled usage are a constant drain on shared properties. Our smart electricity meters give body corporates, HOAs and centre managers a live, accurate picture of consumption per unit or tenant.

Every meter reports remotely, so billing is precise and disputes over usage become a thing of the past — while unusual spikes are flagged before they turn into a nasty surprise on the next statement.
