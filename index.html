Frequently Asked

Common questions

Is the site assessment really free?
Yes. We conduct a free, no-obligation site inspection for every enquiry so we can give you an accurate, honest quotation.
Do you work with bodies corporate and HOAs, not just individual owners?
Absolutely — most of our installations are for complexes, estates, blocks of flats and shopping centres where sub-metering solves shared billing disputes.
Who manufactures the meters you install?
We are the official marketing and installation partner of Precision Meters for smart water metering and PUCo for smart electricity metering.
Can I monitor usage remotely?
Yes — every installation includes IoT remote monitoring so you can view consumption data securely from your phone or computer.
I don't have installation experience — can I still join as an installer?
Yes. Our installer programme includes full training. We're actively recruiting and training new installers as demand grows across South Africa.
